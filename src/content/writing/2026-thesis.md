summary: "This qualitative single-case study examines how one school's leadership responded to an external accreditation report, and whether accreditation worked as a tool for school improvement. Its core finding concerns a gap between two steps: launching initiatives in response to accreditation feedback, and verifying whether those initiatives produced measurable improvement. That gap is the question I now bring to my work as a CIS evaluator and as a member of my school's response team for academic action plans."
